Stage 1 of 3 · Sprite bank review

IRONFRONT

A real-time strategy game for the Commodore 64. Every frame below is the exact cell data the generator writes to sprites.asm, drawn with the C64 palette at true multicolor pixel shape (2:1 wide). Refer to frames by their ID, such as TANK NE-1, when asking for changes.

On the battlefield

A 320×200 screen at PAL speed (50 frames per second). The player's tank is selected and the cursor is ordering a strike on the enemy tank. Ore crystals and terrain here are placeholders for the character-set work in Stage 3.

Vehicles & aircraft

Eight headings, two track frames each. N, NE and E are hand-drawn. The other five headings are mirrored or flipped copies, so a fix to a drawn heading carries over to its copies.

Rifle squad

One hardware sprite carries a whole squad. As the squad takes damage it drops from three soldiers to two to one, and the survivors regroup around the same centre point. Every strength has the same 15-frame layout, so the game only swaps the base frame.

Explosion

Six frames. The sprite colour changes each frame (white, yellow, orange, red, dark grey) while the shared black and grey carry the smoke.

Cursors & selection

High-resolution single-colour sprites (24×21 at full pixel width) for crisp edges on top of the multicolor battlefield.

Hardware notes & open questions

How the bank is wired up on the VIC-II, and the decisions to settle before Stage 2.

Colour setup

  • $D025 = 0 black: outlines, track gaps, windows.
  • $D026 = 1 white: barrels, tracks, cargo, canopies, metal.
  • Sprite colour $D027+n = faction colour, so one bank serves both armies.
  • Cursors are hires sprites. Explosions use a fixed per-frame colour from sprite_colours.

Memory

Settled in review 1

  1. Colours: brown ground, yellow player, blue enemy, white metal.
  2. Unit scale stays at about 14×16 px for tanks.
  3. Squads thin out from 3 to 2 to 1 soldiers.
  4. Helicopters added in the side-on style of the reference.
