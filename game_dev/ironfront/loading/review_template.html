Stage 2 of 3 · Loading screen review

IRONFRONT

The title picture the C64 shows while the game loads. It is a multicolor bitmap: 160×200 wide pixels, and in every 4×8 block the black background plus at most three other colours. What you see below is decoded from the exact bytes in loading.kla.

The screen

A dusk battlefield. Your gunship comes in low on the right, a tank pushes forward, and a rifle squad charges past an explosion, silhouetted against the fire.

How it is built

Every element is generated by gen_loading.py, so each one can be changed on its own and the picture rebuilt.

Your helicopter

Its blue-greys map by brightness to black, dark grey, grey and light grey.

Scene layers, back to front

  1. Sky dithered from night black through blue and purple to a red horizon, with a few stars.
  2. Ridge line and dirt, darker toward the viewer and lit light red around the blast.
  3. Explosion: a fireball from white through yellow and light red to orange and red, with a smoke column blowing right.
  4. Helicopter from your art.
  5. Rifle squad as black silhouettes, and the player tank in faction yellow.
  6. The IRONFRONT logo across the top, and the PRESS FIRE TO DEPLOY prompt.

On the machine

  • loading_screen.prg shows the picture, plays the title march, and waits for fire on joystick port 2 or SPACE.
  • Bitmap at $2000, screen RAM at $0400, border and background black.
  • In Stage 3, pressing fire will go straight into the game.

Title march

A D minor military march at 125 BPM for the three SID voices. It opens with two bars of snare rolls, then a sixteen-bar theme in two halves that loops for as long as the screen is up.

This preview runs the real 6502 music driver in an emulator, then plays the SID register writes it produced through a simplified SID model in your browser. The rhythm, notes and instruments are exact. The real 6581 or 8580 chip sounds fuller and grittier, especially the filter, so the final check is on the C64U.

Instruments

    On the machine

    • Driver and song are 1,351 bytes at $1000, called once per frame from a raster interrupt on line 250.
    • At most raster lines of CPU time per frame.
    • A one-frame hard restart before each note gives every attack the same clean bite.
    • ironfront_title.sid plays on its own in any SID player, including the C64U's.
